# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local residents and visitors in Ankleshwar, Gujarat — primarily dessert lovers and coffee drinkers aged 18–45 looking for a quality cafe experience. Their context: casual outings with friends/family, takeaway orders, discovering what's new in town.

## Product Purpose

1224 Dessert Cafe exists to bring premium desserts and coffee to Ankleshwar's GIDC neighbourhood — a quality experience without needing to go to a big city. The website's purpose is to showcase the menu and atmosphere, provide essential information (location, hours, contact), and drive in-store visits and WhatsApp orders.

## Brand Personality

Refined · Welcoming · Confident. A dessert cafe that's elevated but not pretentious, warm but not cute. Speaks with the quiet confidence of a gallery — the desserts are the art.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark cafe sites (dark wood, exposed brick, moody lighting, barista-core)
- Pastel cute dessert shops (pink/peach, whimsical fonts, cupcake icons)
- Overdesigned corporate templates (glassmorphism, hero metrics, 3D renders, gradients)
- Any site where AI design tropes are obvious (side-stripe borders, gradient text, numbered section markers, tiny uppercase trackers on every section)

## Design Principles

1. **The dessert is the art.** Every layout choice frames the product. Generous whitespace, gallery-like presentation, no clutter.
2. **Confident restraint.** Minimum ornament, maximum impact. Thin red line where others would use a full border. 300-weight italic where others would bold. Volume comes from negative space, not decoration.
3. **Warmth through texture, not colour.** The palette stays architectural (white, near-white, ink) — warmth lives in the red accent, the cream highlight sections, and the physical descriptions of the food.
4. **One coherent voice across all pages.** The same typographic system (Cormorant for display, Space Grotesk for nav, Inter for body, Space Mono for prices) runs through every page. No surprises.
5. **Digital as a physical space.** The site should feel like walking into the cafe — airy, clean, intentional. Load states, hover effects, and transitions should feel tactile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 at all times
- Large text (headings, display) ≥ 3:1
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected: fade transitions, no bounce
- Links visibly distinct (underscore on hover/active, not colour-only)
- All form labels correctly associated
- Semantic HTML throughout
